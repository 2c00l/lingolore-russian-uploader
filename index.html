LingoLore Russian Uploader is a local Windows application used by the owner of LingoLore Russian to publish original language lessons on YouTube.

What the app does

  • Uploads prepared videos to the owner’s YouTube channel.
  • Sets video titles, descriptions, thumbnails and visibility.
  • Adds English, Russian, Spanish, French and Brazilian Portuguese caption tracks.
  • Checks upload processing and resumes interrupted uploads using the existing video ID.

Why Google access is requested

The app uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API. YouTube’s youtube.force-ssl permission is needed to manage uploads and caption tracks. Although Google’s permission description includes deletion, this app’s publishing workflow does not delete videos, ratings or comments.

The owner approves access in Google’s consent screen. The app checks the selected channel against @LingoLoreRussian before uploading or changing a video.

Runs on the owner’s computer

Google tokens and Jellypod credentials are stored locally, encrypted with Windows account protection. This website is informational: it has no sign-in form, upload form or access to those credentials.

For details about information use, retention and revoking access, read the Privacy Policy. This tool is for the channel owner’s personal use and is not offered as a public upload service.
