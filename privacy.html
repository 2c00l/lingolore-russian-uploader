Privacy Policy

This policy describes the personal Windows tool used by the owner of the LingoLore Russian YouTube channel. It also describes this informational website.

Information the app accesses

With the owner’s Google authorisation, the app accesses the selected YouTube channel’s identifier and basic channel information, video metadata and processing status, and caption-track identifiers and status. It sends the videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and subtitle files that the owner has prepared for publication.

OAuth access and refresh tokens allow the app to make these requests. The app does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or calendars.

How information is used

Information received from Google is used only to verify the intended channel, upload and manage the owner’s videos and caption tracks, verify processing, and recover interrupted uploads. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

LingoLore Russian Uploader’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where information goes

Videos, metadata, thumbnails and caption files are sent to YouTube through Google’s APIs. They become visible according to the visibility setting chosen by the owner. Google handles that data under its own policies.

Jellypod is used separately to generate narration from scripts supplied by the owner. Google OAuth tokens and information retrieved from YouTube are not sent to Jellypod by the app. This informational website does not receive app credentials or YouTube account data.

Storage and retention

Credentials and resumable upload-session information are encrypted locally using Windows DPAPI and are bound to the owner’s Windows account. Video assets, metadata, subtitle files, upload identifiers and completion records are kept in the local project folder until the owner removes them.

There is no hosted database of Google users. The application is used only by the channel owner. Local project information may be inspected by the owner or an assistant explicitly authorised by the owner to operate or troubleshoot this workflow.

Revoke access or remove data

The owner can revoke the app’s Google access at Google Account connections and remove the locally stored credentials and project files from their own computer. Revoking access stops subsequent authorised API use. Removing local files does not remove videos already uploaded to YouTube; the owner manages those separately in YouTube Studio.

Because this is a personal tool, the owner controls all retained local data directly. Questions about the tool can be directed to its owner through the LingoLore Russian channel.

This website

This site contains no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels or submission forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security purposes as described in its Privacy Statement. Links to Google and YouTube open those services, which have their own privacy policies.
